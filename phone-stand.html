Phone Stand

Presentation for ENME272 and analysis of 2nd rendition created with a Fusion360 generative design study

Cellphone Stand Presentation - Matthew Shelton ↗

Some design details not mentioned in the slideshow:

  • The stand is supposed to be under 20 grams when 3d printed (PLA)
  • The holes on the "wings" of the stand are intended to provide the option of screwing the stand into a workbench or table, allowing it to hold onto larger devices like small tablets that may topple such a light stand when not fixed to something
  • It was printed with a gyroid infill pattern which is shown to do well with forces in many directions (which the stand must withstand as shown by the free body diagram in the presentation)

3D Model created in Solidworks and embedded using Google Model Viewer through a Github page. Designed for an iPhone 17 but will work with other similarly sized phones.
